Nasa

The National Aeronautics and Space Administration (NASA) is the United States government agency responsible for the civil space program, aeronautics research, and space exploration

NASA is leading the United States civilian space program, including historic achievements like landing the first humans on the Moon during the Apollo program.

What is NASA?

It is a civilian agency of the United States government.
It handles space exploration and airplane research.
It started on October 1, 1958.
Its headquarters are in Washington, D.C.
